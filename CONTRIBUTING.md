# Contributing

Thank you for helping! In order of how much they help right now:

1. **Translations.** If you speak Oshikwanyama or Oshindonga (or another
   Namibian language), add sentence pairs to [community/](community/README.md),
   or review someone else's. Test-set pairs need a second speaker.
2. **Open data.** Know of a text that exists in a Namibian language and in
   English and is openly licensed or in the public domain? Open an issue with
   a link and where its licence is stated. Before writing a download script,
   check [docs/data.md](docs/data.md): it lists what has been checked and
   rejected.
3. **Permissions.** Publishers of Oshiwambo material (newspapers, NGOs,
   ministries) can release text under an open licence. If you have contacts,
   say so in an issue.
4. **Code and experiments.** Improvements to the pipeline, and results on
   open test sets.

## Rules for data

- Only openly licensed or public-domain data goes into this project's
  datasets. Record the licence, and where it is stated, in the dataset's
  `INFO`.
- Never commit data files, splits, checkpoints or result files. The
  download scripts are the record.
- No output of commercial translation services or LLMs, and no data
  generated by models trained on non-open data.
- Test sets are human translations and stay out of training.
